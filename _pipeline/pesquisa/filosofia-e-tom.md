## Bloco 2 — A ausência de karma e de justiça cósmica

*Bloco rico, mas com uma distinção que precisa ser feita na nota final:*

- **Karma como mecânica existe** na obra: há um `karma divine tree` (killer move de
  caminho da madeira, caps. 1516–1740), karma ligado ao `rule path` (cap. 1482), e um
  mestre que diz a um discípulo "every cause has an effect" (cap. 1409). Isso é
  causalidade rastreável e manipulável por Gu — **não** é justiça moral.
- **Karma como justiça moral não existe.** As passagens abaixo são a evidência.

### A declaração central: não há justiça no céu e na terra

O capítulo 1820 chama-se literalmente **"No Justice in Heaven and Earth"**. É o discurso
filosófico mais completo da obra sobre o assunto — Fang Yuan, dentro de um `dream realm`,
desmonta a crença do líder do clã Chen no `justice Gu`:

> "Let me ask you, when you slaughtered Nie clan, was it an act of justice? Even if they really had a plan to destroy our mountain, from their perspective: You are a murderer trying to slaughter their whole clan, by resisting you and defending their homes and family, wasn't that justice too?"
> — cap. 1820

> "Now look, both sides had their own perspective of justice, right?"
> — cap. 1820

> "Let me ask you, is it justice that a sheep eats grass?" [...] "From the sheep's perspective, without grass, there would be no food, it would starve to death, it had to eat grass. But from the perspective of the grass, it had worked so hard to stay alive [...] Isn't the grass an innocent victim? Isn't it pitiful?"
> — cap. 1820

> Chen clan's leader shook his head: "Isn't sheep eating grass just a natural thing? I have never pitied the grass because this is how the world is meant to be."
> — cap. 1820

> "Precisely." Fang Yuan nodded: "The truth of this world is the law of the jungle, big fish eats small fish, and small fish eats shrimp, this is what nature entails. What crime can there be? There is no such thing as justice here. Sheep eats grass, humans eat sheep, that has nothing to do with justice, it is just for survival."
> — cap. 1820

> "**There is no such thing as justice in heaven and earth, there is only justice made by humanity.**"
> — cap. 1820

> "Since time immemorial, humans had to unite to gain strength for the sake of survival, in order to live in this cruel world!"
> "Then, how can humans become united?"
> "Through organizations, through law, and through morals."
> — cap. 1820

> "We organize clans or sects, distributing work according to an individual's ability. We use laws to restrain people, to tell people what they cannot do. We use morals to provide direction, to encourage people to do certain things. Mothers are doting and children are filial, neighbors live in harmony, they all follow the rule of justice and morals. Be it intentionally or unintentionally, from the start of time, any organization would always promote this, because organizations follow their own survival instincts."
> — cap. 1820

> "Or rather, the organizations with comprehensive laws, realistic morals, and proper class systems find it easier to survive. After a long time passes, this becomes the norm of society for the future generations."
> — cap. 1820

> [Voz do narrador, fechando a cena] "And now, Fang Yuan told him the answer, justice and morals were man-made creations. This could be either a conscious or a subconscious creation, people follow these guidelines and allow themselves to survive better in a hostile environment, they can stay united as a group for a long time."
> — cap. 1820

> Fang Yuan concluded: "When you understand the core meaning of justice, you will know — this so-called justice is like iron armor, it is a human tool. When you wear it, you need to use it like a tool. But look at you now, this iron armor has become your chain, it is binding you down."
> — cap. 1820

*[Importante para a nota final: a tese não é "a moral é mentira", é **"a moral é uma
tecnologia de sobrevivência coletiva"** — funcional, real e eficaz, mas humana e não
cósmica. E a cena termina com o líder do clã Chen conseguindo usar o `justice Gu`
outra vez — ou seja, o argumento de Fang Yuan **fortaleceu** a justiça dele em vez de
destruí-la. A obra é mais sutil que o niilismo simples.]*

## Bloco 5 — O objetivo do protagonista: vida eterna

*Bloco abundantíssimo. A expressão `eternal life` aparece em **344 linhas** do
texto-fonte; `immortality` reforça o mesmo tema. O objetivo é reafirmado do capítulo 2
ao 2216 — é o motivo condutor da obra inteira, repetido em praticamente todos os
momentos de decisão importante.*
