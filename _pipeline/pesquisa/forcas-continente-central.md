## 0. Correções a notas já existentes no vault

### 0.1 CORREÇÃO GRAVE — Lang Ya **não** é do Continente Central, é das Planícies do Norte

`05 - Sociedade/07 - As Grandes Forças do Mundo.md` lista a Terra Abençoada / Seita de
Lang Ya sob "Outras forças do Continente Central". **A obra afirma o contrário, duas
vezes e de forma inequívoca:**

> "Most critically, Hu Immortal blessed land was in central continent, while **Lang Ya
> blessed land was in northern plains**, even the hole earth Gu was not easy to use."
> — **cap. 656**

> "**Lang Ya blessed land was in northern plains** while Hu Immortal blessed land was in
> central continent. The two were not in the same region so using hole earth Gu was not
> possible." — **cap. 665**

Reforços: a terra abençoada ficava junto ao **Crescent Lake** (cap. 461, 1048, 1051),
lago das Planícies do Norte; depois da guerra ela **se mudou** e a nova posição só é
conhecida pelo espírito da terra (cap. 1085). O que é verdadeiro e explica a confusão:
**o sistema de seita de Lang Ya foi copiado do Continente Central**:

> "Other than this, I have also established Lang Ya Sect! **This sect system will use
> Central Continent as a reference**, I will be the sect's first supreme elder…" — **cap. 900**

**Decisão:** Lang Ya sai da nota do Continente Central e é registrada como força do
Norte; na nota final entra apenas como advertência de atribuição.

### 0.2 CORREÇÃO — a Doca das Miríades de Dragões não é "a mais forte e prestigiada das dez"

A nota 07 afirma isso. O que a obra diz é mais interessante e mais instável:

- Depois que Song Zi Xing desertou e derrotou os oito imortais mandados para matá-lo,
  "Myriad Dragon Dock went into a downturn, its reputation was even lower than that of
  Immortal Crane Sect. **It became the worst among the ten great ancient sects.** Only
  until now, when Vicious Lightning Fiend came out of closed cultivation, did they start
  to regain their status." — **cap. 848**
- No presente do cenário: "There are stronger and weaker sects even among the ten great
  ancient sects. **Immortal Crane Sect is weaker than Myriad Dragon Dock**, this is known
  by everyone." — **cap. 839**
- Quem está de fato no topo é a **Casa da Afinidade Espiritual**, por causa de Feng Jiu Ge:
  "Spirit Affinity House recruited Feng Jiu Ge, **allowing them to be at the lead of the
  ten ancient sects in this generation**. The other nine sects faced some suppression in
  one way or another." — **cap. 944**

**Decisão:** o topo da hierarquia atual é a Casa da Afinidade Espiritual; a Doca das
Miríades de Dragões é a seita em ascensão rápida, saindo do fundo.

### 0.3 AJUSTE — a especialidade do Templo do Céu Negro **é** declarada

A nota 07 diz "Especialidade não declarada". A obra dá um golpe de assinatura:

> "Hmph, are you saying that my Black Heaven Temple's **signature immortal killer move,
> defending day and night**, cannot conceal our presence and would be seen through
> easily?" — **cap. 811**

Ou seja: ocultação/defesa. Coerente com a doutrina de preservação de forças (cap. 2044).

### 0.4 AJUSTE — a Seita do Lótus Celestial não é "cura e recuperação"

A nota 07 atribui "cura e recuperação". Não achei base textual para isso. O que a obra
afirma: fundada por **Genesis Lotus Immortal Venerable**; o primeiro ancião supremo
**Chen Yi** é **rank 8 de caminho da madeira** e herdeiro da herança verdadeira de
Genesis Lotus (cap. 1522, 1709, 1760); e a seita tem **cinco Casas de Gu Imortal**, o
maior número entre as dez (cap. 1708, 2333). **Decisão:** o eixo é caminho da madeira +
karma + frota de Casas de Gu Imortal.

## 1. O que é o Continente Central, politicamente

### 1.1 A região das seitas, por decreto de um Venerável

> "Our Central Continent is different from the other four regions. **Led by Primordial
> Origin Immortal Venerable, Central Continent had already been reformed during the
> Remote Antiquity Era, establishing the sect system.** During the Remote Antiquity Era,
> sects had been rare. In Olden Antiquity Era, clan forces surpassed sect forces. In
> Medieval Antiquity Era, sects and clans clashed against each other, and advanced at an
> equal pace. In Late Antiquity Era, clans weakened while sects flourished. And now,
> **sects stand in great numbers in Central Continent while there are almost no clans**."
> — **cap. 704**

Como ele fez isso, na prática — e este é um detalhe delicioso de worldbuilding:

> "The first rank nine Gu Immortal in human history is known as Primordial Origin Immortal
> Venerable. **He was very aware of the drawbacks of the clan system, and to initiate a
> sect system, he set an example himself, setting up astrolabes in many places. As long as
> someone was able to walk six steps on the astrolabe chessboard, they could become his
> personal disciple.**" — **cap. 915**

## 5. CIDADES E INSTITUIÇÕES QUE NÃO SÃO SEITAS

### 5.1 Emperor City

> "**Emperor City was the number one city of humanity, it used a vast number of mortal Gu to build.** Even though the city had **relocated a dozen times**

> "this Emperor City was an important location of Central Continent, **it had human path arrangements that attracted and nurtured generations of elites.
> With the accumulation over time, there was an abundance of human qi here, it was the greatest breeding ground of Gu Immortals.**" … "**Emperor City
> produced many talented people, they were all geniuses with abundant luck.**" — **cap. 1727**

É uma **casa de Gu mortal**, não imortal, mas com dispositivos de caminho humano embutidos: "even though **Emperor City is a mortal Gu House, it contains
human path methods**, at the crucial moment, immortal killer moves will protect us all" — **cap. 1717**. Sede da **final** da Convenção do Caminho de Refino
(cap. 1707–1727), protegida por três linhas de casas de Gu imortais das seitas (cap. 1708).

### 5.2 Divine Emperor City

Terra sagrada do **caminho humano**, dentro do território do Tribunal Celestial. É simultaneamente cidade habitada — "**All kinds of people lived in Divine
Emperor City, there were even rank six Gu Immortals**" (cap. 2064) — e Casa de Gu Imortal: "**This Immortal Gu House contains countless people, it is filled
with the profundity of human path. It can actually relocate the human vein gathering point with ease!… Divine Emperor City gathers the strength of all the
people inside, be they Gu Immortal, Gu Master, or mortal, they are gathered together and used in unison.**" — **cap. 2063**. Criada por **Genesis Lotus
Immortal Venerable** e **guardada pela vontade dele** (cap. 2010, 2064). Contém os **mundos de pintura** que "**can take in the city residents and return them
safely after a period of time**" (cap. 2064). O cargo de **Senhor de Divine Emperor City** se conquista herdando a herança verdadeira de Genesis Lotus dentro
do mundo de pintura (cap. 1992). Atacá-la = enfurecer o Tribunal e mobilizar o continente inteiro (cap. 1981).

### 5.3 Tian Ti Mountain — a montanha das heranças

> "**Tian Ti mountain became the holy land of central continent's Gu Masters, a place closest to the immortal court. Many Gu Masters seeked for immortality
> but failed, and before death, they would place their tombs here. At the same time, a large majority of them would also set up their inheritances here.**"
> — **cap. 341**

> "**Everyone might know Tian Ti mountain is the stairs that connects to the heavenly court. Even though it was destroyed and had been abandoned for many
> years, it still represents the might of the heavenly court. In a way, attacking Hu Immortal blessed land is attacking Tian Ti mountain; and attacking Tian

**A regra de acesso que define a região:** "even if I were in central continent, **I am not a disciple of the ten great sects and thus cannot participate in

compete in a fair manner.**" — **cap. 335**.

> "**Reincarnation battlefield was the publicly recognized number one battlefield killer move in the world, it was set up by Paradise Earth Immortal Venerable

Custo permanente: "We have already invested a huge amount of resources into the Reincarnation Battlefield, two Gu Immortals were already involved in it…

stationed there, was heavily injured, he had no choice but to return to the sect to recuperate." — **cap. 745**.

### 5.5 A Convenção do Caminho de Refino

> "**This Refinement Path Convention is not a normal event, but a grand occasion that occurs only every one hundred years in Central Continent. That means,
> without special lifespan prolonging methods, most Gu Masters can only participate once in their lifetimes.**" — **cap. 828**
> "**Central Continent's Refinement Path Convention was created by the ten great ancient sects, but secretly, Heavenly Court was supporting it.**" — **cap. 829**
> "**there were hundreds of sign up locations all over Central Continent. Most of them were sects similar to Five Virtues Sect, having abundant strength and
> being closely linked to the ten great ancient sects, or simply one of their representatives.**" — **cap. 829**
> "**this Refinement Path Convention was held by the ten great ancient sects of Central Continent, they would favor their own region.**" — **cap. 829**
> Formato: quatro tarefas de inscrição e token (cap. 829); a partir da sétima rodada, **um único vencedor por sede** (cap. 839); final em Emperor City.
> Efeito cultural: "**Every time Central Continent held the Refinement Path Convention, it would create a trend of Gu refinement flourishing everywhere in
> Central Continent.**" — **cap. 832**

### 5.6 A corte imperial — desambiguação obrigatória

Não existe "corte imperial" do Continente Central. A ***Imperial Court blessed land*** é das **Planícies do Norte** (cap. 696, 1114) e a ***Merman Imperial
Court*** é do **Mar do Leste**. O que faz papel de corte no centro é o **Heavenly Court** (Tribunal Celestial); e há, **dentro** da sede dele, um salão chamado
*Immortal Emperor Court* (rota central da planta, ver Atlas). **Emperor City** é cidade mortal, não corte de governo.

## 6. COMO SE ENTRA NUMA SEITA DO CENTRO

- **Exame público de admissão** é a porta padrão; a Seita do Combate Imortal usa **dez vagas entre cem candidatos, decididas em combate** (cap. 1227).
- **Escada e periodicidade:** discípulo externo → interno → de elite → de legado → ancião de seita (rank 4) → líder de seita (rank 5) → ancião supremo (rank 6+),
  com exames intermediários de **oito anos** e o "grande exame" para legado (cap. 334, 335).
- **Aptidão:** numa das dez, **grau C simplesmente não existe** e o grau A é comum (cap. 335).
- **Recomendação por imortal** também abre porta (cap. 1227: "since you were recommended by my senior brother").
- **Pontos de contribuição** e ranking público, com resgate por Gu, empréstimo de Gu Imortal por tempo, acervo e financiamento de refino: o exemplo detalhado da
  obra é o da Seita de Lang Ya (Norte), que **copiou o sistema do Continente Central** (cap. 900, 1029, 1032, 1042, 1077).
- **Ancião supremo externo** é o cargo que absorve gente de fora sem diluir a casa: Bo Qing vira "external supreme elder" da Casa da Afinidade Espiritual ao se
  converter (cap. 552).
- **Ser reconhecido como força subsidiária** é a porta institucional para um grupo inteiro entrar no bloco correto — inclusive um grupo suspeito: "I have entered
  the righteous path faction using **the status of a subsidiary force**" — **cap. 709**; e a cláusula que o torna negociável: "**Furthermore, this subsidiary can
  detach from you at any time.**" — **cap. 702**.
